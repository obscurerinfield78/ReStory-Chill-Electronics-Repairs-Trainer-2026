## 🎮 About the Game

Released on August 6, 2026, ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs is a single-player narrative shop-management and simulation game developed by Mandragora and published by tinyBuild. Set in mid-2000s Tokyo, it follows the operation of an electronics repair shop specializing in nostalgic consoles, phones, music players, and other Y2K-era devices.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs**. This trainer enhances the repair-shop experience by providing optional support for shop finances, spare-part inventory, repair workflows, and device restoration tasks. It is designed for players who want to explore customer stories, branching choices, and workshop progression with less pressure from resource management or repetitive repair steps.

**Target Audience:** For players who enjoy cozy simulation games, electronics restoration, Y2K nostalgia, branching customer stories, and shop management but want a more relaxed approach to money, parts, and repetitive repair tasks.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Shop Funds** — Keep the workshop supplied with money for spare parts, purchases, licenses, and shop improvements.
- **Unlimited Spare Parts** — Prevent selected repair components from being consumed when replacing damaged or missing parts.
- **Free Parts Purchases** — Remove the financial cost of ordering replacement components through the in-game browser.
- **Repair Workflow Assist** — Reduce or compatibility repetitive repair interactions during disassembly, cleaning, replacement, reassembly, and testing.
- **Soldering Assist** — Make soldering tasks easier by simplifying the associated repair challenge.
- **Firmware Repair Assist** — Streamline device reprogramming tasks used to resolve software-related faults.
- **Instant Device Restoration** — Complete supported restoration steps quickly while preserving the normal customer-order and shop-management flow.
- **No Combat** — ReStory has no combat system; the trainer focuses exclusively on repair, inventory, finances, exploration, and story progression.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the repair shop and surrounding areas from a first-person perspective. Move between the counter, workbench, storage, cleaning equipment, tools, and other interactive stations. Use the trainer only after the game has finished loading into the shop and avoid activating options while changing locations or entering story scenes.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory management is central to the repair loop. Store spare components, salvage usable parts, organize incoming devices, and order replacements through the Y2K-era browser when necessary. The unlimited-parts option helps maintain a reserve of common components, while free-purchase support reduces the cost of restocking.

### World & Environment

ReStory takes place in the bustling streets of mid-2000s Tokyo. Run the shop, accept in-person and online orders, improve the business, meet customers, and make dialogue choices that influence their personal stories and the game's multiple endings. Use financial features carefully if you want to preserve the intended progression and decision-making.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat in ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs. Gameplay revolves around diagnosing devices, disassembling hardware, cleaning components, replacing faulty parts, soldering connections, reprogramming devices, reassembling repairs, serving customers, and managing the shop.
